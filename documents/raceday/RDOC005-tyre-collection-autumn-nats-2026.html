01 Welcome

Introduction

Welcome to the ROK Cup South Africa Autumn Nationals 2026. This document covers everything you need to know about tyre collection — what tyres to use, when to use them, and how to collect them for race day.

Using the correct tyres is essential to keeping the competition fair. It ensures every driver competes on equal footing. This guide will walk you through the approved tyre specifications, when they come into play over the race weekend, and the exact process for collecting your allocated race tyres.

Equal Competition

By following these guidelines, you help maintain the integrity of the series and give yourself the best chance to perform at your peak. Read through the details carefully, make sure you are set, and get ready to race with confidence.

03 Procedure

Race Tyre Collection

Collecting Your Tyres

Tyre collection will open at the times listed on the official schedule. You will need to present your race number and may be asked for proof of entry. Ensure you arrive at the tyre collection point on time and ready to receive your allocation.

Tyre Control

  • All tyres will be recorded at collection.
  • Do not remove or tamper with the markings — this will be treated as a technical infringement.
  • Tyres must be fitted before your first timed session. No changes are allowed unless approved by a scrutineer.

Lost or Damaged Tyres

Lost Tyres

Lost tyres will not be replaced unless specifically authorised by the Clerk of the Course.

Damaged Tyres

Tyres damaged in transit or before fitting must be reported immediately at the tyre desk. Do not attempt to use a tyre you believe may be defective.

Missed Collection Slot

Do Not Miss Your Collection Time

If you miss your designated collection time, you may not be able to receive your race tyres. Without the correct tyres, you will not be permitted to take part in qualifying or any subsequent racing. It is your responsibility to ensure timely collection. No exceptions will be made.

04 Rules

Tyre Usage

Qualifying & Heat Tyre Rules

The race tyres issued to you must be used from qualifying onwards, and must remain on your kart for all heats.

  • National entries must enter parc fermé for qualifying with brand-new tyres.
  • This ensures a fair and level playing field across all competitors.
  • Regional-only drivers at National events may use a used set, but these tyres must be presented and scanned before qualifying.

Post-Session Scanning

After each session — qualifying and all heats — your tyres will be scanned by officials. This confirms that the tyres on your kart match the ones issued to you.

Incorrect Tyres = Exclusion

Incorrect tyres found at scanning will result in exclusion from that session or heat. There are no exceptions to this rule. It is your responsibility to ensure the correct tyres are fitted at all times.

05 Schedule

Collection Times

All competitors are required to consult the official event timetable and ensure they are present at the designated tyre distribution point at their scheduled time. Racing slicks will be issued strictly according to this timetable, and it is essential that these tyres are fitted to your kart prior to the start of qualifying.

As per regulations, all competitors must begin qualifying on new slicks. Missing your allocated collection window may result in tyres not being issued to you, and without the correct tyres you will not be permitted to take part in qualifying or any subsequent racing.

Your Responsibility

There will be no exceptions or alternative collection times. Please take responsibility for knowing your slot, arriving on time, and being prepared. Check the official event programme for your class-specific collection window.

  • Check the official event timetable for your class-specific collection slot.
  • Arrive at the tyre collection point ready to receive your allocation.
  • Have your race number and proof of entry available if requested.
  • Fit your tyres and present your kart to scrutineering before your first session.
