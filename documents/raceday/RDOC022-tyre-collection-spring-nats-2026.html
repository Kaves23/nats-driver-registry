Instructions

Spring Nats 2026 · Killarney International Raceway · 10–12 September 2026

01Collection

Collection Process

Tyre collections must be completed by the designated team representative during the official collection window. Drivers and teams must keep all tyres labelled and staged correctly for inspection.

ItemAction
Collection windowAttend during the official tyre station hours only.
DocumentationPresent race number, team name and the required tyre allocation form.
HandlingCheck tyre markings, count and condition before leaving the collection point.
ReturnAny damaged or non-compliant tyre must be reported immediately.
02Rules

Important Rules

  • Do not remove or alter tyre markings before inspection.
  • Do not exchange tyres with another team without official approval.
  • Retain all tyre paperwork until the event is complete.
  • Any discrepancies must be raised before the next session begins.
Reminder

Late collection or poor tyre handling can affect your correct tyre allocation and your ability to compete within the event schedule.
