01Placement

Sticker placement rules

All required stickers must be applied in the exact positions directed by the event officials. Poor placement may lead to a failed scrutineering or delayed track access.

  • Apply only to clean, dry surfaces that are free from oil, dirt and moisture.
  • Place each sticker squarely and without wrinkles, folds or overlap.
  • Ensure the sticker is visible and not obscured by bodywork or wiring.
  • Do not peel or reposition stickers once attached unless directed by officials.
