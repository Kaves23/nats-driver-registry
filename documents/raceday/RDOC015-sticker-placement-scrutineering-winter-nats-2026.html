02 Access Control

Armband Regulations

Each competitor envelope contains armbands for the mechanic appointed to be inside Parc Fermé and pre-race with the driver. Armbands are scanned and registered — they are your access pass to the controlled area.

Wearing Requirements
  • Armbands are strictly non-transferable
  • Must be worn at all times on Friday, Saturday and Sunday
  • If only one armband is supplied, it must not be removed over night
  • No replacement armbands will be issued under any circumstances on Saturday or Sunday
Loss of Armband
  • As all armbands are scanned and registered, the loss of an armband will result in the competitor being unable to access Parc Fermé
  • This renders the competitor ineligible to participate in the event
  • No replacements will be issued — treat your armband with extreme care
Failure to Comply

Failure to comply with armband regulations will constitute a breach of event entry conditions and will result in exclusion from the event.

03 Checklist

Scrutineering Readiness Checklist

Before presenting your kart for scrutineering, ensure the following are in order:

  • All required sponsor logos applied in the correct positions as per the diagram above
  • Self-declaration scrutineering form (RDOC007) completed and signed
  • Transponder fitted correctly — under bib, behind front axle
  • All safety gear (helmet, suit, gloves, boots) compliant and in good condition
  • Armbands worn by the driver and designated mechanic at all times
  • Engine seal number and engine number recorded on your self-declaration form
