Race Day Instructions

Spring Nats 2026 · Killarney International Raceway · 10–12 September 2026

01Overview

Professional Race Standards

OK National drivers must treat every session as a controlled operation from paddock to grid. Correct preparation, efficient communication and discipline in pit lane are mandatory.

  • Check in with officials and confirm the session schedule before leaving the paddock area.
  • Ensure all compliance paperwork and fuel declarations are complete and signed before the first call.
  • Be ready for practice, qualifying and race sessions with the kart in a fully legal and functional condition.
02Checklist

Team Readiness

Safety Full race-approved safety equipment worn correctly and accessible.
Mechanical Alignment, steering, brakes, chain and axle integrity checked on the kart.
Numbers Number boards fitted cleanly and legibly in the correct position.
Compliance Fuel and tyres checked and paperwork presented before grid access.
03Procedure

Operational Discipline

  • Only approach the grid when the team manager confirms it is safe to move the kart.
  • Report any kart issue or technical concern immediately to race control or the relevant marshal on duty.
  • Follow all grid, pit lane and post-session instructions without exception.
  • Ensure a clear and speedy handover between sessions and maintain full attendance for briefing windows.
Final Reminder

The OK National class should be treated as a professional racing environment. Prepared teams, aware drivers and disciplined controls are the foundation for a safe and efficient weekend.
