01 Welcome

Introduction

Fuel is one of the key elements in making kart racing fair, competitive, and safe. To keep things equal across the board, a controlled fuel system is in place for the ROK Cup South Africa. The principle is straightforward: everyone uses the same fuel, from the same source, mixed the same way.

This is not just about levelling the playing field — it is about removing the guesswork and grey areas around performance. The racing must be decided by the driver and the kart setup, not by what is in the tank.

Applies To

Controlled fuel applies to all classes except Cadet. Controlled fuel will be used for the full race weekend, from the first practice session through to the final. All teams and drivers in applicable classes must follow this procedure without exception.

Everything you need to know about how and where to collect the fuel, what the mix needs to be, and how compliance will be checked is set out in this document. Not following it could lead to penalties or disqualification. Please read it carefully and ensure your whole team is across the requirements.

02 Step-by-Step

Fuel Procedure

Please follow the steps below carefully for every session throughout the event weekend.

  1. 1
    Fuel Tank Drop-Off
    All fuel tanks must be dropped at the fuel station — a clearly marked and signed area on circuit. Before drop-off, ensure your tank has your name, race number, and required fuel level clearly written on it using a marker. Remove any old markings from previous events first.
  2. 2
    Collect Your Tank
    Collect your tank with enough time to prepare for your upcoming session. Do not fit or remove tanks at the fuel station — collect your tank and move to your designated pit area before carrying out any work on your kart.
  3. 3
    Fit the Tank
    Fit the tank to your kart in your own pit area. Ensure all connections are secure and no fuel is spilling or leaking. The tank must be fitted correctly and safely before you take to the circuit.
  4. 4
    After Your Session — Return Immediately
    Return your tank immediately after each track session. Late returns may lead to penalties. Your tank must be handed back to the fuel station promptly after you come off the circuit.
Do Not Leave Parc Fermé With Your Tank Attached

Under no circumstances may a competitor leave parc fermé with the fuel tank still attached to the kart after a session. This will lead to disqualification from the event. Remove the tank and return it to the fuel station before leaving the controlled area.

03 Rules & Penalties

Compliance & Penalties

The controlled fuel system relies on every team following the procedure correctly and consistently. Officials will monitor and enforce compliance throughout the event weekend.

Key Compliance Requirements

  • Only the official controlled fuel may be used — no private fuel from any source.
  • Fuel tanks must be clearly labelled with name, race number, and fuel level before drop-off.
  • Tanks must be collected and returned at the designated fuel station only.
  • Do not tamper with or modify the fuel or fuel tank in any way.
  • The tank must be returned immediately after every session — no exceptions.
  • Karts must not leave parc fermé with the tank still attached after a session.

Fuel Checks

Officials reserve the right to conduct fuel checks at any time during the event weekend. Samples may be taken at the fuel station, in parc fermé, or at any point during the event. Competitors must cooperate fully with any official fuel inspection request.

Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to follow the controlled fuel procedure — including use of unauthorised fuel, late tank returns, or leaving parc fermé with the tank fitted — may result in time penalties, exclusion from a session, or disqualification from the event. The decision of the stewards is final.

Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to the ROK Cup South Africa standards. Following this procedure ensures the racing remains fair for everyone involved. If you are unsure about any aspect of the fuel procedure, please speak to an official at the fuel station before your session.
