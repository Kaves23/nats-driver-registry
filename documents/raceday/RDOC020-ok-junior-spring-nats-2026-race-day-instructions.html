OK Junior
Race Day Instructions

Spring Nats 2026 · Killarney International Raceway · 10–12 September 2026

01Overview

Pre-Session Readiness

OK Junior drivers must comply with all technical, sporting and circuit instructions before entering the grid or pit lane. Timing is critical and any delay in readiness may result in missed sessions.

  • Report to the paddock control point and confirm team registration before the first official call.
  • Have the kart fully prepared with compliance checks completed before the timeline begins.
  • Ensure all required paperwork, driver gear and team communications are complete before the first session.
02Checklist

Required Checks

Driver Race suit, gloves, balaclava, helmet, boots and approved safety equipment.
Mechanical Steering, front-end alignment, chain tension, braking and wheel security inspected.
Tyres Legal compounds fitted, labelled and compliant with scrutineering instructions.
Fuel Controlled fuel and declaration compliance completed before track access.
03Procedure

Track Access

  • Only move the kart to the grid when directed by officials or the team manager.
  • Report any abnormal noise, steering, brake or tyre issue immediately to the paddock steward or clerk.
  • Remain attentive to grid and pit lane instructions; listen for calls from race control and marshals.
  • At the end of each session, return the kart to the designated area and confirm the next requirement with your team.
Key Reminder

All OK Junior competitors are expected to arrive prepared, keep communication open with the team, and complete scrutineering and compliance procedures before the opening session.
