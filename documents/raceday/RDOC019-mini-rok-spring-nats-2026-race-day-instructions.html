ROK Cup SA

Mini ROK
Race Day Instructions

Spring Nats 2026 · Killarney International Raceway · 10–12 September 2026

01 Overview

Race Day Requirements

All drivers must be present and prepared before the start of the official practice and qualifying windows. Mini ROK operations are tightly controlled, and the on-time arrival of drivers and chassis is critical.

  • Arrive before the official call time listed in the event bulletin and keep all equipment ready for inspection.
  • Present the kart in a fully ready state with complete number panels, fuel, and tyres as required.
  • Check with the team manager or clerk of the course before leaving the paddock area.
02 Checklist

Driver & Kart Checklist

Driver Race suit, gloves, helmet, balaclava, approved shoes, ID and team contact details.
Kart Number boards fitted, front and rear lights connected where required, steering and brakes checked.
Tyres Tyres stamped and fitted correctly, no illegal compounds or non-compliant pressures.
Fuel / Scrutineering Fuel system sealed as instructed and self-declaration completed before track access.
03 Process

On-Track Procedure

  • Follow all grid, pit lane and marshal directions exactly. Do not enter the circuit until signalled by the clerk or officials.
  • Report immediately to scrutineering if any issue is discovered with the kart, numbers, tyre condition or fuel system.
  • Ensure you are ready for the green flag and respond to briefings without delay.
  • Any off-track mechanical issue must be reported to the race control desk before re-entering the session.
Important Reminder

Drivers must remain with the team and be available for checks, briefings and grid communication. Delays caused by incomplete preparation may affect track access or race participation.
