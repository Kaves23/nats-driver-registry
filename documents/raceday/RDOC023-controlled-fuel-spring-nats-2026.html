Controlled Fuel
Instructions

Spring Nats 2026 · Killarney International Raceway · 10–12 September 2026

01Fuel

Fuel Rules

All fuel handling must be completed in accordance with the official event instructions. Controlled fuel is mandatory where specified, and any discrepancy must be raised before the next session.

  • Use only the approved fuel type and approved supply methods stated by the event.
  • Only refuel at the designated station under supervision, when instructed.
  • Do not add or remove fuel from the tank outside the approved process.
  • Present the supplied documentation for duty checks if requested by officials.
02Process

Operation

  • Make sure the vehicle is correctly positioned before the fuel team attends.
  • Confirm the fuel quantity and required paperwork with the team manager or official before releasing the kart.
  • Keep the fuel cap area clean and accessible to the appointed official.
  • Any fuel issue must be reported immediately to the race control desk or fuel station attendant.
Important

Any attempt to bypass the controlled fuel process or use unapproved fuel may result in technical penalties and loss of session compliance.
