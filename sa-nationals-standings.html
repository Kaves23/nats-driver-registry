Drop-heat rule: for each weekend (2 rounds), the single lowest eligible individual heat score is dropped — eligible meaning a genuine scored result, or a zero from a round the driver did not enter at all. A heat cannot be dropped if it was an exclusion/DSQ/DNS within a round the driver otherwise raced. The dropped heat is shown struck-through in red; the Sub column shows the adjusted round total with the drop amount.
